**Analysis**

`parse_money` does binary float math on decimal input, then truncates:
- `float("12.34") * 100` → `1233.9999999999998` → `int(...)` → **1233**, not 1234. Same for `"1.15"`, `"4.35"`, `"70.07"`, and many other common amounts.
- `int()` truncates toward zero rather than rounding, so `"-1.15"` → `-114` while `"1.15"` → `114` — asymmetric behavior around zero.

`clamp` has no ordering precondition and inherits `min`/`max` NaN semantics.
