FINDING: app/utils.py | `parse_money` truncates toward zero rather than rounding, so sub-cent input like "12.345" silently becomes 1234 with no error or documented rounding policy.

FINDING: app/utils.py | `parse_money`'s truncation is asymmetric for negative amounts (refunds/credits): "-12.345" → -1234 rounds *up* in magnitude terms while positive values round down, producing inconsistent, non-reconciling totals.

FINDING: app/utils.py | `parse_money` performs no input validation or error handling — `None`, `""`, `"$12.34"`, or `"12,34"` raise raw `ValueError`/`TypeError` out of a "parse" helper instead of a meaningful error or documented contract.
